## Open licensing question

**Svg.Custom 3.2.1 is under the Microsoft Public License (MS-PL)**, which the Free Software Foundation lists as
incompatible with the GNU GPL. It is not a direct dependency: Mapsui (the map engine) uses Svg.Skia, which uses
Svg.Custom. The maintainers have recorded this and will decide how to resolve it before a release (for example an
additional permission under GPLv3 section 7, or another licence for Ketras). Until then, do not redistribute Ketras
binaries.

## Protocol definitions and generated code

- The MAVLink message classes in `src/Ketras.Mavlink/Messages/Generated` are generated by
  `tools/mavlink-generator` from the MAVLink message definitions (common dialect), which are MIT-licensed by the
  MAVLink project.
- `tests/Ketras.Station.Tests/Fixtures/px4-sitl-actuators.json` is the actuator metadata generated by a PX4 SITL build
  (PX4-Autopilot, BSD-3-Clause), used only by tests.
- `src/Ketras.Station/Setup/ActuatorCondition.cs` is adapted from QGroundControl's actuator condition parsing
  (`src/Vehicle/Actuators/Common.cc`, v5.1.4), © QGroundControl Project, GPL-3.0 (dual-licensed with Apache-2.0).

## Data and services

- **Satellite base map:** Esri World Imagery. Credited on the map as "Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS
  User Community". Use is subject to Esri's terms of use.
- **Street base map:** OpenStreetMap tiles from `tile.openstreetmap.org`. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, under
  the Open Database License; credited on the map. The tile servers' usage policy applies: Ketras identifies itself
  with a User-Agent and keeps a disk cache; it does not bulk-download areas. Heavy users should configure their own
  tile source (a custom URL in Settings).
- **Terrain:** Copernicus DEM GLO-30, downloaded on demand from the Copernicus DEM public bucket. Credited in the
  planner and the 3D view as "Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH
  2014-2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA".
- **Vehicle metadata:** parameter and actuator descriptions come from the connected vehicle (or the download address it
  names) at run time; nothing is bundled.
